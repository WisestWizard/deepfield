DEEP FIELD · Cheat sheet

DEEP FIELD at a glance

For DEEP FIELD 1.8, checked against its source.

An ambient drone machine for the M-VAVE FM-1. Each key starts a voice, a note that swells in and keeps sounding. KNOB 1–4 shape every voice at once, and PLAY sets them moving.

Getting around

tapHOME
HOME page: TIMBRE, DRIFT, SUB, TREMOLO on KNOB 1–4. Reverb lives on FX and the FX mixer; every preset comes with it on.
turnSELECT
The next or previous page.
tapEDIT
OSC: model, shape, sub, noise (shape and noise named for the model). Turn SELECT for NOISE, again for NOISE 2 (type, gust, duck, width).
tapENV
ENVELOPE: attack, release, aftertouch, glide. Turn SELECT for LEAD.
tapLFO
LFO: tremolo rate, vibrato (to 40 cents either way, DRIFT its rate), random, width. Turn SELECT for VOICES: spread, cross mod, timbre drift, pan drift (what makes eight voices sound like eight).
tapFX
ENSEMBLE. Turn SELECT for DELAY, DELAY 2, REVERB.
tapARP
GEN. Turn SELECT for GEN 2, whose knobs are the mode's too — a knob the mode never reads is empty.
tapGLO
MIX: BED (everything latched), LEAD (what you play over it), OUT (a trim under the MASTER pot). Turn SELECT for FX MIX (ensemble, delay, reverb, shimmer on one screen), then GLOBAL (scale, root, morph, TEMPO — the arp and a synced delay time both follow it), MIDI and USB.
turnMASTER
Volume: the speaker, the headphones and the USB recording.

Playing

tapany key
Start a voice: it swells in and keeps sounding (LATCH on, SEL lit). How hard you strike it is its weight: the level over 18 dB, how bright it is, and how fast it arrives (VELOCITY, on by default; off, every key strikes full). A firm press is already a full strike: the keybed counts its hardest 15 % as full.
tapits lit key
Stop that voice; it fades out as you let go, however slowly you pressed. If GEN moved it, its light moved too: tap the key it sounds on.
holdits key+turnKNOB 1–4
That voice alone: LEVEL (−24 to +6 dB, or MUTE), TONE (its own TIMBRE), TUNE (±50 cents), LOCK (ON: GEN and the arp leave it be). Several keys held: all of them. Saved with the preset.
press harder ona held key
Aftertouch: brighter, with more vibrato (at the LFO's own rate), while you press. The top seven keys have no aftertouch.
tapSEL
LATCH off: the voices keep sounding, and each key plays a lead over them while you hold it. Tap again for LATCH on.
holdSEL+tapkeys
Move the chord: each key moves the whole latched chord there, its lowest note to that key. Let go of SEL and the keys play as before.
holdSEL+tapHOME
Stop every voice. SEL held is the chord's button and has no timer: held with nothing touched, it does nothing.
tapOCT-OCT+
The keys an octave down or up, two each way.

Shaping the sound

turnKNOB 1
On HOME, TIMBRE: dark to bright.
turnKNOB 2
On HOME, DRIFT: how much each voice wanders.
turnKNOB 3
On HOME, SUB: an octave below, on every model. The same value as SUB on OSC.
turnKNOB 4
On HOME, TREMOLO: each voice slowly fades down and back up.
turnALGORITHM
The model: ADDITIVE, SUPERSAW, FM, BOWED, FORMANT, BELL, SHEPARD, WAVEFOLD, WAVETABLE (wavetables), FM 4-OP (four-operator FM), SYNC (hard sync), TAPE (a worn tape loop), RESONATOR (a singing bowl); every voice crosses over smoothly. TIMBRE, SHAPE and NOISE are named for what they do on each (CUTOFF, VOWEL, PATCH …).
tapEDIT+turnSELECT
NOISE: wind, rain or crackle under the voices.

GEN: the voices move

tapPLAY
GEN on or off. PLAY's colour shows the mode. Turning it on opens the GEN page (KNOB 1 the mode, KNOB 2–4 set it; GEN 2 stays if open); turning it off leaves the page be. ARP opens the page any time.
holdSEL+tapPLAY
The next move now (ARP: the next step), and the countdown starts over.
WALK
One voice at a time moves to a nearby note.
SHIFT
The whole chord shifts along the scale, one voice after another. RETURN is how hard home pulls.
RANDOM
Several move at once, each its own way.
ARP
An arpeggio through the voices' notes, the first step of each bar accented. On GEN 2, EUCLID puts rests between the notes (3/8, 5/16 …) and DIV sets the step (1/16 to a bar).
PROG
The chord is led through a progression over the scale you set, one or two voices at a time while the rest hold (nothing is frozen — LOCK a voice yourself if you want a pedal under it): ROUND, UNDERTOW (sinks), SLOW RISE (climbs), TWO ROOMS, OPEN DOOR, COLOUR, BREATH, STONES on KNOB 4. On GEN 2, ROAM develops it: 0 plays it as written, up redraws about one chord in seven and moves the key centre.
LOOP
A phrase comes back at the velocities, the rhythm and the note lengths you played it with — hold a note for a second and it comes back held for a second. Selecting LOOP with nothing recorded says REC THEN PLAY: press REC on this page to record one (LATCH lifts for the take, REC again closes it and it loops at once, at the speed you played it), or just play with LATCH off and your last eight notes are caught on their own. DECAY fades it and at 0 holds it for good, recorded or caught (turned up, a caught phrase dies faster than a recorded one), OCT JUMP throws notes an octave, ROAM on GEN 2 carries each pass to another degree.
turnKNOB 2left
RATE down to 0.1 s: below a second GEN plays the chord instead of changing it.

Effects

holdFX+tapwhite key 1–4
Ensemble, delay, reverb, shimmer on or off, on any preset. Switched off, the delay and reverb ring out.
holdFX+tapwhite key 5
The noise layer on or off.
tapSEQ
FREEZE: the reverb holds what it has. Tap again to let it go.
tapFX+turnSELECT
DELAY: type, time, feedback, mix — a DLY TIME move takes the repeats out for 12 ms and lands them on the new time, so it no longer swoops (TAPE still slides, inside a tone and a half). DELAY 2: tone, diffusion, wow, gate. REVERB: decay, damping, shimmer (the octave up read twice, one a side, so it is as wide as the hall), reverb.

Presets and saving

turnPRESETS
Change the sound: the screen lists the presets and a line fills; then it blends in over MORPH (4 s) under your chord. LATCH, GEN and FREEZE stay. PRESETS alone never brings a chord.
holdSEL+turnPRESETS
The preset's chord comes too, in place of yours: the old voices release as the new swell (factory chords stand on C and play in ROOT). SCALE changes to the scale the chord was written in.
holdHOME1 s
The preset back as saved: its sound, effect switches and GEN values blend back in; your chord keeps playing.
tapSAVE+turnPRESETS+tapSAVE
Save the sound and its chord (each voice's own values too) to U1–U16. HOME cancels.

MIDI and Bluetooth

in overUSBTRSBluetooth
Notes (any channel by default), and clock to time GEN.
out overUSBBluetooth
The keys and GEN's moves. The TRS jack is in only.
tapGLO+turnSELECT+turnALGORITHM+tapREC
Connect a Bluetooth MIDI keyboard: the MIDI page shows CONNECTED or NOT CONNECTED and lists the devices it finds; ALGORITHM picks one, REC connects it, REC again disconnects. On other pages REC searches first.
holdREC1 s
Bluetooth on or off.
tapGLO+turnSELECT
MIDI: in and out channels, local, velocity (on by default). Turn again for USB: recording and clock out.
senda CC
Every knob has one, in and out: 16–19 TIMBRE DRIFT REVERB TREMOLO, 21 SUB, 9 MODEL, 109–113 GEN's MODE RATE CHANCE RANGE RETURN, 15 CHORDS, 51 ROAM, 54 BED, 2 SCALE, 3 ROOT … (the manual has the table). A knob turned sends its CC; a CC in never lights SAVE.
sendCC 57–63
Switches: 57 LATCH, 58 GEN, 59 FREEZE, 60–63 ensemble, delay, reverb, shimmer (64 up is on); 12 the noise layer. Triggers: 13 the preset as saved, 14 GEN's next move now.
sendSysEx
F0 7D 44 46 01 64 F7 asks for every user preset; each comes back as a frame you can keep, share, and send back into a U slot. tools/fm1_bank.py dumps and loads a bank.
sendprogram 0–31
Load the sound of F1–F16, then U1–U16 (never a chord).
sendNRPN
A voice's own values, in and out: CC 99 = 1 LEVEL, 2 TONE, 3 TUNE, 4 LOCK; CC 98 = its note; CC 6 = the value.

Good to know

  • A button always opens the same page, and SELECT steps between pages. GLO opens the two mixer pages first, then the scale and the rest. The four boxes at the bottom of the screen are the four knobs.
  • SEL, PLAY and SEQ are lit while their mode is on: LATCH, GEN, FREEZE.
  • F1–F16 are the factory presets, every model once and every GEN mode on two (press PLAY to hear it); F16 INIT is every value at its start. U1–U16 are yours.
  • Switching on, the name falls into a vortex for three seconds (press anything to skip). Then it is silent: the last preset's sound is ready, and nothing plays until you press a key.
  • Knobs: turned slowly, small steps; turned fast, big ones. A knob with a few values moves one a click. A value glides to where you turn it and a choice fades across, so turning never clicks.
  • Key lights: bright is a voice sounding there (tap to stop it), half is sound going (a voice fading, the held chord while LATCH is off), a glow is the scale.
  • On the screen only sound gives off light. Sun means latched, white your hand (a lead, an open voice), cyan where you are, teal an effect on, orange something to look at.
  • The settings (scale, octave, MIDI, Bluetooth on or off) are stored only when you save a preset. SAVE is lit while a sound or a setting is unsaved.
  • SEL is a button (LATCH) and SELECT is a knob (pages): different controls.
  • The keys send the velocity you struck them at over MIDI, whatever VELOCITY says; that switch only decides whether DEEP FIELD itself listens. A factory preset's chord is struck full, and its balance against the rest of the bank sits in each voice's LEVEL.
  • Update mode: hold REC while you switch on. Hardware calibration (buttons and knobs): hold OCT- and OCT+ while you switch on.
  • Back to the stock firmware: the installer puts M-VAVE's own back.
