DEEP FIELD at a glance

For DEEP FIELD 0.6, checked against its source.

An ambient drone machine for the M-VAVE FM-1. Each key starts a body, one voice that swells in and keeps sounding. KNOB 1–4 shape every body at once, and PLAY sets them moving.

Getting around

tapHOME
HOME page: MASS, DRIFT, SPACE, TIDE on KNOB 1–4.
turnSELECT
The next or previous page.
tapEDIT
BODY: model, shape, sub, air. Turn SELECT for NOISE.
tapENV
ENVELOPE: swell, fade, press, glide. Turn SELECT for LEAD.
tapLFO
MOTION: tide rate, wobble, walk, width.
tapFX
ENSEMBLE. Turn SELECT for DELAY, DELAY 2, REVERB.
tapARP
ORBIT. Turn SELECT for ORBIT 2.
tapGLO
GLOBAL: scale, root, view, morph. Turn SELECT for MIDI and USB.
turnMASTER
Volume.

Playing

tapany key
Start a body: it swells in and keeps sounding (LATCH on, SEL lit).
tapits lit key
Stop that body; it fades out as you let go, however slowly you pressed. If ORBIT moved it, its light moved too: tap the key it sounds on.
holdits key+turnKNOB 1–4
That body alone: LEVEL (−24 to +6 dB, or MUTE), TONE (its own MASS), TUNE (±50 cents), ORBIT (PINNED: ORBIT and the arp leave it be). Several keys held: all of them. Saved with the scene.
lean ona key
Brighter, with more wobble, while you lean. The top seven keys can't sense a lean.
tapSEL
LATCH off: the bodies keep sounding, and each key plays a lead over them while you hold it. Tap again for LATCH on.
holdSEL+tapkeys
Move the chord: each key moves the whole latched chord there, its lowest note to that key. Let go of SEL and the keys play as before.
holdSEL1 s
Stop every body.
tapOCT-OCT+
The keys an octave down or up, two each way.

Shaping the sound

turnKNOB 1
On HOME, MASS: dark to bright.
turnKNOB 2
On HOME, DRIFT: how much each body wanders.
turnKNOB 3
On HOME, SPACE: how much reverb.
turnKNOB 4
On HOME, TIDE: each body slowly fades down and back up.
turnALGORITHM
The model: SOLAR, VOID, GLASS, STRING, CHOIR, BELL, SHEPARD, FOLD, SCAN (wavetables), DX (four-operator FM); every body crosses over smoothly. On DX, SHAPE picks one of eight voices.
tapEDIT+turnSELECT
NOISE: wind, rain or crackle under the bodies.

Orbit: the bodies move

tapPLAY
ORBIT on or off. PLAY's colour shows the mode.
holdPLAY
ORBIT page: KNOB 1 picks the mode, KNOB 2–4 set it.
WANDER
One body at a time moves to a nearby note.
CHORD
The whole chord shifts along the scale.
SCATTER
Several move at once, each its own way.
ARP
An arpeggio through the bodies' notes. On ORBIT 2, RHYTHM puts rests between the notes (3/8, 5/16 …) and DIV sets the step (1/16 to a bar).
CYCLE
The chord walks a progression (I IV V and seven more).
ECHO
Your last eight leads (LATCH off, over a latched bed) come back, quieter each time.
PULSE
Each body dips and swells back in turn: a slow strum.
turnKNOB 2left
EVERY down to 0.1 s: below a second ORBIT plays the chord instead of changing it.

Effects

holdFX+tapwhite key 1–4
Ensemble, delay, reverb, shimmer on or off, on any scene. Switched off, the delay and reverb ring out.
holdFX+tapwhite key 5
The noise layer on or off.
tapSEQ
FREEZE: the reverb holds what it has. Tap again to let it go.
tapFX+turnSELECT
DELAY: type, time, feedback, mix. DELAY 2: colour, diffuse, wow, chop. REVERB: size, damp, shimmer, space.

Scenes and saving

turnPRESETS
Change the sound: the screen lists the scenes and a line fills; then it blends in over MORPH (4 s) under your chord. LATCH, ORBIT and FREEZE stay. With nothing latched, the scene's own chord comes too (factory chords stand on C and play in ROOT).
tapSAVE+turnPRESETS+tapSAVE
Save the sound and its chord (each body's own values too) to U1–U16. HOME cancels.

MIDI and Bluetooth

in overUSBTRSBluetooth
Notes (any channel by default), and clock to time ORBIT.
out overUSBBluetooth
The keys and ORBIT's moves. The TRS jack is in only.
tapREC+turnALGORITHM+tapREC
Find a Bluetooth MIDI keyboard and connect to it. REC again disconnects.
holdREC1 s
Bluetooth on or off.
tapGLO+turnSELECT
MIDI: in and out channels, local, velocity. Turn again for USB: recording and clock out.
sendCC 16–19
MASS, DRIFT, SPACE, TIDE. CC 20 is the noise level, 64 sustain, 123 stops every body.
sendprogram 0–31
Load the sound of F1–F16, then U1–U16.
sendNRPN
A body's own values, in and out: CC 99 = 1 LEVEL, 2 TONE, 3 TUNE, 4 ORBIT; CC 98 = its note; CC 6 = the value.

Good to know

  • A button always opens the same page, and SELECT steps between pages. The four boxes at the bottom of the screen are the four knobs.
  • SEL, PLAY and SEQ are lit while their mode is on: LATCH, ORBIT, FREEZE.
  • F1–F16 are the factory scenes; U1–U16 are yours. F3 GLASS ORBIT and F11 GAMELAN were made for ORBIT.
  • Switching on, the name falls into a vortex for three seconds (press anything to skip). Then it is silent: the last scene's sound is ready, and nothing plays until you press a key.
  • Knobs: turned slowly, small steps; turned fast, big ones. A knob with a few values moves one a click.
  • Key lights: bright is a body sounding there (tap to stop it), half is sound going (a body fading, the bed while LATCH is off), a glow is the scale.
  • On the screen only sound gives off light. Sun means latched, white your hand (a lead, an open body), cyan where you are, teal an effect on, orange something to look at.
  • The settings (scale, octave, MIDI, Bluetooth on or off) are stored only when you save a scene. SAVE is lit while a sound or a setting is unsaved.
  • SEL is a button (LATCH) and SELECT is a knob (pages): different controls.
  • Update mode: hold REC while you switch on. Hardware calibration (buttons and knobs): hold OCT- and OCT+ while you switch on.
  • Back to the stock firmware: the installer puts M-VAVE's own back.
