# DEEP FIELD

DEEP FIELD is an ambient drone machine for the M-VAVE FM-1. Every key strikes a body that swells in and stays:
eight of them orbit a sun on the screen, breathe with the tide, drift, and move to neighbouring notes on their
own when ORBIT runs. Four forces on the knobs shape them all at once; a frozen reverb and a shimmer carry the rest.
MIDI in and out over USB, Bluetooth (both ways) and the TRS jack.

## Play it in your browser

No FM-1 needed: [open DEEP FIELD in your browser](emu/). Headphones help. Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all
work; on a phone, turn it sideways.

1. Press Switch on. The scene FIRST LIGHT loads and its notes swell in.
2. Click a few keys, one at a time, or type them: Q to I and A to K are the white keys, 1 to 0 and the minus key
   the black ones. Each note swells in and stays.
3. Click a lit key again: that note fades out.
4. Drag KNOB1 to KNOB4 up or down: the brightness, the wander, the space and the tide of every note at once.
5. Press PLAY (or the P key): the notes start moving to neighbouring notes on their own.
6. Turn PRESETS to change the scene; the one you stop on morphs in over a few seconds.
7. Hold SEL for a second to let every note go.

The [manual](manual/) has the rest.
